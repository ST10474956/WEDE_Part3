Why Second-Hand Tablets Make Smart Sense

Tablets can cost a small fortune when bought new — but the value drops fast. That’s why savvy South Africans are turning to certified pre-owned devices. You get the same great performance for a fraction of the price.

Every tablet we sell goes through strict quality checks: screen, battery life, charging ports, and cameras. Customers know exactly what they are getting with full transparency.

Refurbished Laptops for Students

With more schools and universities going digital, students need affordable laptops. Our refurbished laptops start from just R3000 and are ideal for online learning, projects, and research.

Popular brands: Lenovo, Dell, HP, Acer, and Apple MacBooks.

E-Waste Awareness Campaign

In partnership with local NGOs, Tech Collective is running an e-waste drive. Customers can drop off old or broken devices, and we’ll recycle or refurbish them responsibly. Together, we can reduce harmful waste and protect the environment.

Upcoming Online Store Launch

We’re excited to announce that Tech Collective will soon launch a fully functional online store! Customers will be able to browse, compare, and order second-hand devices from their phones or computers.
