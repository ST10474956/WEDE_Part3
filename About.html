About Us

Our Story

Tech Collective was founded in 2023 by a group of friends who wanted to make technology more accessible while reducing electronic waste. What started as a small project fixing old laptops and phones has grown into a trusted business serving students, families, and small businesses.

Our Mission

To make technology affordable and reduce e-waste by reselling quality second-hand devices. We believe in giving electronics a second life while helping our customers save money.

Our Vision

To be a trusted marketplace where people confidently buy reliable second-hand technology, while supporting a greener and more sustainable future.

Our Values

  • Affordability: Quality devices at fair prices.
  • Sustainability: Reducing e-waste and protecting the environment.
  • Trust: Honest product descriptions and reliable service.
  • Innovation: Finding creative ways to reuse and recycle technology.

Our Customers

We proudly serve:

  • Students who need affordable laptops and tablets.
  • Small businesses looking for cost-effective solutions.
  • Families on a budget.
  • Eco-conscious buyers who want to reduce waste.
